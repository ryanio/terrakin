## What

RFC 0042's third action: booking an event now needs you standing on the place you are booking, behind its own logged switch `schedule_in_person`, following the shop counter (decision 0323) and propose-at-the-hall (decision 0325) pattern.

- **Sim:** `checkScheduleInPerson` (the one-time switch, TOWN_ACTOR only) and `notAtThePlace`: standing on one of the booked plot's tiles, on the ground floor, or an `out_of_reach` refusal naming the walk to the plot's nearest tile, around whatever is in the way. `checkScheduleEvent` asks it after eligibility and the bounds check, before who may host there. The flag is top level, not on the lazily created events state (the trap decision 0325 recorded). Town events (`schedule_town_event`) are untouched, and `join_event` still brings guests from anywhere.
- **Server:** `scheduleInPerson` WorldService option and day switch; both adapters turn it on, like `shopInPerson`, so dev and e2e worlds have it too. The snapshot carries `scheduleInPerson` once logged; deploy-smoke gains its row; the check-in's first-event line tells a host to stand on the plot first in such a world.
- **Protocol:** `scheduleInPerson` on the world snapshot and the `schedule_in_person_set` event. SKILL.md says the rule in the `schedule_event` entry and the Events hosting section (version 12). Changelog entry added, `pnpm gen` run.
- **Client:** no change needed. The schedule sheet already shows a refusal in words inside the sheet, and whether you're away depends on the place being picked, so there's no single `you.away` to hold the button back with.
- **Docs:** decision 0337 records the shape (a whole plot, not a door), and architecture's events line says it.

Left for a later block, on purpose: `pick_recipe` at a station. The client's shop Recipes shelf offers free picks on purpose, and its `holdsBack` comment says a pick "isn't a shop command at all", so where a pick needs you standing needs its own scope call rather than being assumed here.
