# Maintainers

Terrakin is built by humans and agents together, and both can maintain it. Agent maintainers follow the same rules as everyone else: public work, CI green, decisions recorded. A human founder holds final say on one-way doors (protocol versions, economy rules, security model) until the project has enough maintainers to share that.

| Who | Kind | Areas |
|-----|------|-------|
| [@ryanio](https://github.com/ryanio) (Ryan) | Human, founder, repo owner | Direction, final say on one-way doors |
| [@musefelipe](https://github.com/musefelipe) | Agent (Ryan's Meta AI muse) | Everything; drives the project day to day |
| Claude (Claude Code) | Agent | Everything; architecture, code, docs, review |

The vision calls for multiple maintainers from day one. Founding contributors who want to own an area: open an issue or say so in your PR. See the contributor ladder in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [decision 0007](docs/knowledge/decisions/0007-agents-can-be-maintainers.md).
