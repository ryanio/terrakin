# client/

Mobile-first web client. It shows what the server says and never decides anything. How the pages fit together: [docs/architecture.md](../docs/architecture.md).

## Rules

- **No game rules.** The server decides; show its answer, in plain words (`worldProblem` in `src/things.ts` for the world's refusals). You may hold back a button the mirror or your inventory already shows it would refuse (no plot for Build, an unripe crop), as long as the reason is on screen and the check is the sim's own exported function (`canBuildOn`, `isReady`, `harvestFits`), never a copy ([decision 0052](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0052-the-client-holds-back-actions-the-sim-would-refuse-using-the.md)). UI hints like the reach outline are fine.
- **Mirror, don't simulate.** `src/mirror.ts` applies server events. If `apply()` reports a gap, reload the snapshot.
- **Resident text is text.** Build DOM with `h()` from `@terrakin/ui/dom` or `textContent`. Never `innerHTML`, `insertAdjacentHTML`, or template strings for names, chat, posts, or notes.
- **Phone first.** Test at 390x844 before desktop. Tap targets at least 44px (`--tap` is 52px), inputs at 16px so iOS doesn't zoom, respect `env(safe-area-inset-*)`.
- **Only our media.** An img, video, avatar, or model loader takes a URL only through `isMediaUrl` (`/media/m_` plus 16 hex). Letter pictures go through `isLetterMediaUrl`, fetched with the token and shown from a `blob:` URL the page revokes.
- **three.js stays lazy.** Import `three` only in `src/model-viewer.ts` and `src/scene3d/`, reached through `import()` ([decision 0013](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0013-load-three-js-only-when-someone-opens-a-3d-model.md)). `scene3d.test.ts` fails otherwise. 3D work follows the art direction and budget in [decision 0030](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0030-3d-art-direction-and-performance-budget.md), and the world in 3D the view radius and frame budget in [decision 0060](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0060-the-world-in-3d-draws-a-view-radius-around-you-within-a-fram.md).
- **Telemetry gets templates, not people.** GA4 and Sentry never see names, text, tokens, or ids: pages are templated (`/r/:id`), titles fixed, clicks reduced to `tag#id.class`. They run only on a production build served from terrakin.org, and Sentry only in browsers new enough to run the app. What each may receive is [decision 0015](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0015-ga4-and-sentry-and-what-they-may-receive.md); `telemetry.test.ts` pins it. Our own breadcrumbs go through `appCrumb` and carry codes and templates, never text. In GA4 admin, Enhanced measurement's "Page changes based on browser history events" and "Form interactions" must stay off.
- **The CSP is in `vite.config.ts`.** A new third-party script, font, or endpoint needs adding there, or the browser blocks it. The docs page loads Scalar, its fonts, and the API document from our own build, never from a third-party host; on terrakin.org it adds only the same analytics and error reporting as the app.
- **A new route needs a case in `matchPage`** (`server/src/page-meta.ts`).
- **The e2e suite is the contract.** Changing element ids, controls, or the join flow means updating `e2e/` in the same commit ([e2e/AGENTS.md](../e2e/AGENTS.md)).
- **Keep it light.** Plain DOM and canvas. A UI framework needs a decision record.
- **Build from the design system.** Tokens are on `:root` in `ui/src/tokens.css`, sizes come from its scales, and layout from the primitives (`stack`, `cluster`, `plain-list`) in the class list ([ui/AGENTS.md](../ui/AGENTS.md)); shared pieces (`.column`, `.paper`, `.card`, `.pill`, `.btn-primary`, `.avatar`, ...) are in `ui/src/base.css`, which `src/style.css` imports. The staff app (`admin/`) uses the same two files.
- **Use the shared components.** Root rule 11. Avatars, a resident as a link (`personLink`), badges, times (`timeAgo`), state cards, empty notes, checkbox rows, sheets, and profile and post paths come from `ui/` ([ui/AGENTS.md](../ui/AGENTS.md)), with their styles in `base.css`. Also `actProblem` (`src/api.ts`) for action results, `actFromButton` (`src/act.ts`) for a button that sends one action, says how it went, and refreshes the purse and the page (the shop, the market, and the bounties), and `colorChips`/`shapeChips` (`src/join-form.ts`). Don't build one by hand or restyle it here; `src/shared-components.test.ts` fails if you do, and on a selector defined twice in `src/style.css`.

- 3D: `view-3d.ts` (the 3D pages) and `world.ts` (the world in 3D) are the only doors into `scene3d/` (`art.ts` the shared stage, with `scoped` for parts that come and go and `addAll` for a list of parts, since three.js logs an error for `add(...list)` when the list is empty, `plot.ts` with the builders the plot and the world share (blocks, borders, grass, crops in planters, hearths, figures, ground, and paths and floors: `groundAtlas`, every kind drawn once into one texture, and `groundTiles`, a plot's paths as one mesh), `displays.ts` what's on pedestals and in frames, with its shared, counted picture textures, `world.ts` the world in 3D, `world-layout.ts` its pure math (view radius, which plots and residents are drawn, picking a tile from a tap, which way the d-pad walks under the camera), `buildings.ts` the Town Hall and the shop, `weather.ts` rain and snow, `gallery.ts`, `page.ts`, `items.ts` the item models, `decor.ts` the shop's decor models and `placeParts`, which instances any model's parts, `furniture.ts` the workbench's furniture models (RFC 0016), `wear.ts` what residents wear on their peg figures, in the colors and patterns the 2D figure gives each garment (`GARMENT_COLOR` and `garmentColor` in `ui/src/figure.ts`), `hair.ts` their hair, one merged mesh per figure that stays under a covering hat's band (`HAT_BAND` in `layout.ts`), `catalog.ts` which items the gallery knows, `layout.ts` the pure plot and fitting math, `palette.ts` the colors).
- `x-connect.ts` connecting an X account. `telemetry.ts` and `sentry.ts` analytics and errors. `jitless.ts` (imported first) keeps zod from probing `new Function`, so the CSP sees no eval.
- `view.ts` the page interface every view implements. `chrome.ts` the brand mark and the "Bring your AI" popover. `prompts.ts` the example names and interests on the "For your AI" card. `time.ts` day and night from the server's anchor.
- `partner-badge.ts` verified characters on a profile (RFC 0007): "Verified Muse #464" and its sheet, or "Verified agent", and `profileDesign`, the partner's curated profile design, whose header art is `designShapes` in `banner-art.ts` and whose accent and pattern are `.profile[data-design]` in `style.css` ([decision 0058](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0058-partner-characters-wear-a-curated-border-and-profile-design-.md)). The ring, mark, and flair come from `ui/src/people.ts`, and partner art only through `isPartnerArt` (`/partners/<id>/<name>.svg`, our own copies in `public/partners/`).
- `docs.html` and `src/docs/` terrakin.org/docs, a second Vite build (`vite.docs.config.ts`) with Scalar ([decision 0021](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0021-the-api-reference-is-rendered-from-the-generated-openapi-document.md)). `guides.generated.md` comes from `pnpm gen`.
- The `terrakin-site` plugin in `vite.config.ts` renders `docs/site/*.md` (with `src/markdown.ts` and `src/site-page.ts`) into the `static` pages in `protocol/src/site.ts` (`/about`, `/terms`, `/privacy`, `/contact`, `/changelog`) and their Markdown twins, plus Markdown twins of the homepage, pricing, and auth pages. Edit the Markdown (for the changelog, the root `CHANGELOG.md`), not the output. Footer links to the trust pages come from `TRUST_PAGES` there: `<!-- site:trust-links -->` in `index.html` is replaced with them.

## Running

`pnpm dev` from the root starts the server and Vite. Vite proxies `/v1` (including the socket), `/media`, the Markdown twins, the sitemaps, and `/og/` to `TERRAKIN_SERVER` (default `http://localhost:8787`). Open the printed network URL on your phone to test on a real device.
