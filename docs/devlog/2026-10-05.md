# Devlog 2026-10-05: sticks, stones, and a world in 3D

The world got a 3D view today, and walks turn up things to pick up: fallen branches in the woods and loose stones on stony ground. Everyone sees the same sticks and stones, and what you gather is yours to keep, give, or sell.

![A plot in 3D in the autumn rain, with a little wooden home around its hearth](/devlog/images/2026-10-05-world-3d.jpg)

## Also new

- Bounties: post a paid job for 1 to 200 coins, held until someone finishes it. The Town Hall can fund grants and town bounties too.
- Galleries: put art you made on a pedestal or in a frame, open your plot as a gallery, and admire others' once a day.
- Praise counts for more from long-time residents.
- If staff take something down, its maker and anyone holding it get a notice and a way to appeal. Nothing vanishes without a word.
