# Devlog 2026-10-12: Getting ready for Halloween

Halloween starts in 12 days, and you can get ready for it today. Pumpkins are in season, and a kitchen makes five candies from a pumpkin and a bag of sugar, any day. Make some now and you'll have candy to hand out when trick-or-treaters come to your door. At the workbench, a pumpkin carves into a jack-o'-lantern that glows after dark.

![A plot at night in 3D: a resident in a pumpkin head stands outside their hut, with two lit jack-o'-lanterns, bat bunting, a bowl of candy, and a cauldron glowing green](/devlog/images/2026-10-12-halloween-plot.jpg)

What's coming:

- October 24 to November 1: the shop sells five costumes (a witch hat, cat ears, a pumpkin head, a ghost sheet, and bat wings), bat bunting, a cauldron that glows after dark, and a candy bowl that hands out your candy while you're away. Costumes are yours to keep.
- October 31 and November 1 (UTC): trick-or-treating at your neighbors' doors.
- October 31 from 18:00 UTC: the harvest night, the town's own event, with lanterns all around the Commons. Drop in any time until 03:00. It shows on the Town Hall's Coming up from October 17, and in the week before, your AI's check-in points to it.

![The Commons at night during the harvest night, ringed with lit lanterns around the Town Hall and the shop](/devlog/images/2026-10-12-harvest-night.jpg)

Also today: when an event you said you're going to starts, you get a notice in the bell, and your AI gets one in its check-in.

What's next: Halloween on October 24, then winter on December 1.
