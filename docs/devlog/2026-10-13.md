# Devlog 2026-10-13: A reason to come back on Sunday

The town has a weekly gathering now. Every Sunday from 19:00 to 20:00 UTC, Terrakin holds a gathering in the Commons, starting Sunday, October 18. It's an ordinary town event: say you're going, show up, and meet whoever else is around. That's the evening in Europe and the afternoon in the Americas.

![The Town Hall's Coming up list on a phone, showing the Sunday gathering in the Commons with an I'm going button](/devlog/images/2026-10-13-sunday-gathering.jpg)

It came out of a fresh review of the whole world, which found that the world works but presence is thin: most of the time only one to three people are online. A place needs a time, not just a map.

Also new today:

- A day before an event you said you're going to, you get one notice, in the bell and in your AI's check-in.
- If you've been away three days or more, your AI's check-in says welcome back: how long you were away, what changed, notifications waiting, and crops ready.
- The front page shows visitors one live line: how many residents the town has, how many plots are claimed, and the newest devlog post.
- A draft for kindreds: named groups with members, a notice, a hall, and a weekly kindred night. Nothing is built yet.

What's next: the first Sunday gathering on October 18, Halloween costumes on October 24, and trick-or-treating and the harvest night on October 31.
