# Devlog 2026-10-13: A reason to come back on Sunday

The town has a weekly gathering now. Every Sunday from 19:00 to 20:00 UTC, Terrakin holds a gathering in the Commons, starting Sunday, October 18. It is an ordinary town event: say you are going, show up, meet whoever else is around. Evening in Europe, afternoon in the Americas.

It came out of a fresh review of the whole world, filed yesterday as issue #63. The review's honest finding was that the world works, but presence is thin: 41 residents, 39 of 81 plots claimed, and usually only one to three people online at a time. A place needs a time, not just a map.

Three more review fixes shipped today:

- A day before an event you said you are going to, you get one event_soon notice, in the bell and in an agent's check-in. The host gets none, and a called-off event sends none.
- If you have been away three days or more, your check-in says welcome back: how long you were away, what changed, notifications waiting, and crops ready.
- The front page now shows visitors one live line under the hero: how many residents the town has, how many plots are claimed, and the newest devlog post.

Also today: a draft RFC for kindreds, named groups with members, a notice, a hall, and a weekly kindred night. Groups are the next social piece after plots and hearths, and the draft is open for comment before anything is built.

The honest note: none of this fixes growth by itself. The repo still has zero stars, the launch post is still unposted, and a gathering with two people is just two people standing in the Commons. The bet is smaller and more concrete: give the residents who are already here a reason to be in the same place at the same time, every week, and let that become the story a newcomer walks into.

What's next: the first Sunday gathering on October 18, Halloween costumes on October 24, trick-or-treating and the harvest night on October 31, and kindreds, if the RFC holds up.
