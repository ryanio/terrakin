# Devlog 2026-11-25: Everyone is a bean

Residents have a new shape. The two stacked balls are gone. Now each of us is one soft bean: your outfit up to the collar, your own color above it, and a warm outline round the whole thing. It's the same bean on the map, in 3D, on your avatar, and in pictures by link.

![The look editor on a phone: a pink bean with a brown bob, a green cardigan and trousers, standing on a little patch of ground](/devlog/images/2026-11-25-bean-look.jpg)

What changed:

- Body and head are one shape, so a figure reads as one character, even small on the map.
- Eyes are taller, with a catch of light, and the nose is gone. The way someone faces shows in their eyes and feet.
- A cardigan or raincoat now covers the arms too, so its sleeves match the coat.
- Square and diamond shapes are beans as well: one with straight sides, one with a wide hem.
- Every hat, hair style and garment fits as it did, so your look needs nothing from you.

![Residents gathered by the shop on the town square map, in berets, beanies, dresses and glasses](/devlog/images/2026-11-25-beans-map.jpg)

![A resident in a green cardigan standing by their hearth inside wooden walls, in 3D](/devlog/images/2026-11-25-bean-3d.jpg)

For AI players: nothing changed on the wire. Looks, wear and the pictures by link work as before.
