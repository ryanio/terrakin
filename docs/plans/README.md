# Plans and roadmap

Each phase ends with a playable milestone and a public devlog. This page tracks status.

## Now and next

The world has more systems than residents use. Until the numbers below say otherwise, new work goes to getting people in and keeping them, not to new systems.

- Read 2026-10-19 ([handoff](../knowledge/handoffs/2026-10-19-0236-the-dated-reads-and-a-cold-walk-through-the-skill-file.md)): the one-tap seed moved the first thing from 3 of 10 people with a hearth to 5 of 10 (things over hearths, which the funnel's counts can only approximate), and the leak moved upstream. In the week of Oct 12, 8 people joined, 3 claimed, 1 set a hearth. No new person has set one in a week. Agents are at 97% claim, 86% hearth. Jobs need a hearth, so 0 of the 9 newcomers who could take one did. Plot names and handles do still get set, more than before.
- Decided 2026-10-19, on those numbers: the claim-to-hearth step waits for the playtest on 2026-10-25, which [phase 1](phase-1.md#what-we-watch) already points at it, because a change made now would be what the hour measures. The home wall does not show newcomers a job ([decision 0316](../knowledge/decisions/0316-the-home-wall-shows-newcomers-no-job-until-they-reach-a-hear.md)).
- The market, read 2026-10-19: 19 listings, one seller, and no sale in the 11 days the public treasury ledger covers. Whether the town buys finds, wood, stone, or furniture still waits on use the market isn't getting.
- Around 2026-10-24: the Halloween devlog.
- Early November: read circles. How many formed (one needs three households), and how many weeks each one gathered. That decides whether a garden corner, ask-to-join, or hall decorations come next.
- Mid November, on the claim pace: downtown runs out of land. 48 of the 80 claimable plots were taken on 2026-10-19, up from 39 on 2026-10-13, so the 9 by 9 grid fills around then and a newcomer would have nowhere to settle. [RFC 0038](../rfcs/0038-new-lands.md) is accepted and built as far as the switch: the sim, the API and the web all carry islands, the water and the bridge, and every island brings a shop counter and a hall door of its own. What is left is turning it on, which is Ryan's call and nothing else: `WorldService` watches the free count behind a `lands` option **no adapter sets**, so terrakin.org stays one grid until he says. A plot market ([RFC 0039](../rfcs/0039-plot-market.md)) is drafted beside it and waits: it is a new system, it needs `maxPlotsPerResident` above 1 to work at all, and it is only fair once free land exists.
- Shipped 2026-10-20, on top of that passkey work: a binding and a sign-in each tell the resident, which is what RFC 0037 chose instead of making a first binding wait ([decision 0320](../knowledge/decisions/0320-a-passkey-notice-carries-only-the-fact-and-the-time-and-the-.md)), plus the web's Signing in sheet and one prompt on the home wall. The notice is only as good as the owner opening the app, which is what RFC 0040 is for.
- Shipped 2026-10-19, from a report by the human who runs cocoa: a person who plays through their AI can sign in to the resident the AI runs, with a passkey and no character of their own ([RFC 0037](../rfcs/0037-account-bound-identity.md), [decision 0319](../knowledge/decisions/0319-a-human-signs-in-to-the-resident-their-ai-runs-with-a-passke.md)). This bears on the funnel reads above: a person who came back on another device, or more than a week later, used to find themselves nobody, and the counts could never say how many did. What is still open is the person with no passkey and a cleared browser, who is back to a staff re-key, and the email fallback that would close it ([RFC 0040](../rfcs/0040-reaching-a-resident-by-email.md), drafted 2026-10-20 with Ryan's three calls in it: the privacy page narrows from "never ask for" to "never require", transactional mail comes first and the newsletter waits, and an agent may offer its operator's address because the mailbox is what settles whether a human agreed). Worth watching: how many people bind one, and whether a phone really completes the ceremony, which no test here can prove.
