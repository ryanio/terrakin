# packages/admin/

The staff app at admin.terrakin.org: the review queue, AI triage's suggestions, the moderation log, what the townsfolk are doing, how far newcomers get, and helping an agent that lost its key back in ([RFC 0006](../../docs/rfcs/0006-trust-and-safety.md), [decision 0040](../../docs/knowledge/decisions/0040-a-staff-app-on-its-own-host-behind-cloudflare-access-with-st.md)). It shows what the staff routes answer and decides nothing; the server checks every action.

## Rules

- **Resident text is text.** Reported content, names, report notes, and triage rationales (which can quote residents) reach the DOM only through `h()` from `@terrakin/ui/dom` or `textContent`. Never `innerHTML`, `insertAdjacentHTML`, or a template string into markup.
- **Nothing inline, nothing from elsewhere.** The admin host sends `ADMIN_PAGE_HEADERS` (`packages/server/src/pages.ts`): scripts, styles, fonts, and requests from the same origin only. No inline `<script>`, no `style` attributes, no `data:` fonts, no third-party script, font, or endpoint. No analytics and no error reporting here. The build inlines nothing. `src/jitless.ts` stays the first import in `src/main.ts`, so zod never probes `new Function`, which the policy forbids.
- **Same host, relative paths.** Every call goes to `/v1/admin/...` on the host the app came from, parsed with the protocol schemas (`src/api.ts`). On admin.terrakin.org, Cloudflare Access signs staff in and the Worker passes the email on; the app sends no credentials. Where Access isn't set up, staff paste a resident token, kept in sessionStorage for the tab.
- **Suggestions stay suggestions.** Triage's verdict is shown next to the content and never applied, prefilled, or preselected. A person picks the action and writes the reason. A takedown's "Rule broken" starts on the rule most residents' reports named (`defaultRule`), never triage's.
- **Roles are the server's.** `src/logic.ts` decides what to offer (moderators see suspensions of up to 7 days), but hiding a button is a courtesy; the server refuses anyway. An award needs a staff sign-in, never a staff key.
- **Each screen picks a page layout** from `@terrakin/ui/ui`, as the main app does ([decision 0191](../../docs/knowledge/decisions/0191-every-page-is-a-sidebar-layout-a-wide-card-grid-or-a-column.md)). The queue and bounties are a `pageLayout` with what needs a decision in the main column and the rest beside it, after it on a phone (`sideLast`). Townsfolk is a `pageLayout` with chatter's state and the residents beside what they said. Newcomers is a `wideLayout` with its cards in a `.card-grid`. The log, agents, keys, and the sign-in screen are a `.column`. The top bar and nav are `.column.wide`, so they line up with every layout; on a phone the nav is one row that scrolls sideways. `<main>` is `.admin-main` and adds no width of its own.
- **Every screen is built the same way.** It opens with `pageHead` from `src/view.ts`, the `page-title` and lede the main app's pages have, never a card of its own. A card that holds a form or a panel names itself with a `card-title` inside it; a list of cards gets a `section-title` above it. Its words under a heading are a `field-hint`, a list with nothing in it on the page is a `stateCard`, and one inside a card is a `field-hint` line. Buttons sit in a `cluster` at their own width. Pills are `kindPill`, or `statePill` for where a part of the town stands; lists of like things are `itemRow`s. Whoever did something is named with `actorName` from `src/view.ts`. State cards, people, times, fields, and the toast come from `@terrakin/ui` with their styles ([packages/ui/AGENTS.md](../ui/AGENTS.md)); don't copy them here.
- **Phone first.** Test at 390x844. Tap targets at least 44px, inputs at 16px or more, `env(safe-area-inset-*)` respected. Pictures under review load blurred until tapped.
- **What can't be undone takes two taps.** Deleting files (hiding a post, deleting profile pictures or a piece's picture), taking down a listing, a display, or plot names, suspending, clearing a block, confirming or cancelling a bounty, awarding coins, making a re-key code, merging records, and revoking a key all ask again first (`confirm` in `itemActions`, or `confirmTwice`). Merge stays off until a check passed for exactly the fields asked.
- **Shown once, kept nowhere.** A re-key code, a sign-in link, and a new key are shown once, and the log never keeps the code. The newcomers screen shows counts only and never names anyone.
- **Pure logic is tested.** Anything that decides (actions per item and role, limits, labels, routes) lives in `src/logic.ts` with `src/logic.test.ts`. The flow is covered by `e2e/safety.spec.ts` on `admin.localhost`.

## Where things are

- `index.html` the static shell. `src/main.ts` boots: asks `GET /v1/admin/overview` who is signed in, shows the token sign-in or the staff-only screen, and routes the screens below. Its top bar shows who's signed in (`personLabel`) and their role. `src/jitless.ts` turns off zod's eval probe.
- `src/queue-view.ts` `/`, the review queue: items by target with the author's record, reports, triage's suggestion, and the actions `itemActions` offers, with "Rule broken" on every takedown (`TAKEDOWN_ACTIONS`). Beside it, the Today card (`todayRows`, `triageLine`, `checkinLine`, `spendLine`, `chatterLine`, `participationLine`, `tipsLine`).
- `src/shut-homes-view.ts` the Shut homes card under the queue: hearths with no walk to their shop counter (`GET /v1/admin/shut-homes`), the blocks and walls between tiles that free each (`freesByCost`, `clearWhat`), and a maintainer's check and Clear (`POST /v1/admin/clear-block`, `clearWords`).
- `src/log-view.ts` `/log`, the moderation log, paged with `before`, under day headings.
- `src/townsfolk-view.ts` `/townsfolk`: chatter's state (`chatterState`, `gateWords`), today's calls and spend, and what each townsfolk resident did (`todayWords`, `activityLine`, `ACTION_ICONS`).
- `src/newcomers-view.ts` `/newcomers`: the newcomer funnel (`GET /v1/admin/newcomers`) for all time and each of the last 8 UTC weeks (`NEWCOMER_ROWS`, `stepShare`, `cohortTitle`).
- `src/bounties-view.ts` `/bounties`, maintainers only: town bounties to confirm, running bounties to cancel (`bountyActions`), and Award coins (`POST /v1/admin/awards`, capped by the protocol's `AWARD_RULES`; `awardProblem`, `awardedWords`).
- `src/agents-view.ts` `/agents`, maintainers only: a sign-in link for a person (`POST /v1/admin/signin-links`, `signinMessage`), a re-key code for an agent (`POST /v1/admin/rekey-codes`, `rekeyMessage`), and merging a duplicate record (`POST /v1/admin/residents/{id}/merge`, `mergeSummary`).
- `src/keys-view.ts` `/keys`, every staff role: keys for your own AI (`keyScopes`, `keyMessage`), listed and revoked. Maintainers see and revoke everyone's.
- `src/api.ts` the staff routes. `src/logic.ts` pure decisions and wording (`screensFor`, `itemActions`, `defaultRule`, and the lines above). `src/view.ts` `button`, `outLink`, `pageHead`, `statePill`, `actorName`. `src/style.css` the staff app's own styles, over `@terrakin/ui/tokens.css` and `base.css`.
- `vite.config.ts` builds into `packages/client/dist/_admin/` with base `/_admin/`, after the client build (`pnpm build` runs both).

## Running

- `pnpm dev` from the root starts the server, the client, and this app. Open http://admin.localhost:5174. Vite proxies `/v1` and `/media` to `TERRAKIN_SERVER` (default `http://localhost:8787`) and keeps the Host and Origin headers, so staff routes accept the calls.
- `pnpm start` serves the built app at http://admin.localhost:8787 from the Node server.
- Sign in with the token of a resident listed in `TERRAKIN_MAINTAINERS` or `TERRAKIN_MODERATORS` on the server you started.
