# packages/cards/

Link preview cards (the 1200x630 images X, Discord, Slack and iMessage show for a shared link). satori lays out a template as SVG, resvg (wasm) draws the PNG. The same code runs in a Cloudflare Worker and in Node. [Decision 0028](../../docs/knowledge/decisions/0028-link-preview-cards-and-page-meta-at-the-edge.md) says why.

Depends on nothing in the repo. The server turns API data into a `Card` (`packages/server/src/og.ts`) and serves it.

On Cloudflare this package is a Worker of its own, `terrakin-cards` (`cloudflare/worker.ts`, the root `wrangler.cards.jsonc`), which the main Worker asks for a drawing over its `CARDS` service binding ([decision 0299](../../docs/knowledge/decisions/0299-card-drawing-moves-to-a-worker-of-its-own-reached-over-a-ser.md)). satori, resvg's wasm and the fonts are about 3.5 MB, and the main Worker runs on every request, so they stay out of it. The contract is `CardDrawer` in `src/render.ts`, which both sides use; a `Card` and a `Rendered` are plain data, so both cross RPC as they are. `pnpm cf:deploy` uploads this Worker before the main one, since a service binding to a Worker that doesn't exist is refused.

## Rules

- **Resident text is drawn, never parsed.** Names, bios and posts are string children of `text()` boxes. Nothing here builds markup from them; `svg()` and `img()` only ever take our own markup and data URIs the server checked with `imageDataUri()`.
- **Clean and cut every string a resident wrote.** `drawable()` drops what the fonts can't draw (emoji, scripts outside Latin) and returns undefined when too little is left; `clip()` caps the length. satori wraps text but never shortens it.
- **Keep a render cheap**, about 100 ms in Node. No blurred `boxShadow` (each is a Gaussian blur), no SVG filters per card. The paper grain is rasterized once per process (`render.ts`).
- **Bump `CARDS_VERSION`** in `templates.ts` when a card's look changes, so cached cards are drawn again.
- **Fonts are the static WOFF files from @fontsource** (satori can't read WOFF2). `fonts.ts` lists them for Node and `worker.ts` imports the same files for wrangler; a test keeps the two in step.
- **satori stays pinned at 0.32.0.** Later versions load HarfBuzz from a separate wasm at run time, which Workers refuse. A test fails on a satori that imports it, and `.github/dependabot.yml` ignores satori, so move the pin by hand and only after a card draws under `pnpm cf:dev`. After each deploy, CI's `smoke` job asks the live Worker for three pictures and fails on the redirect to `/og.png` a Worker that can't draw sends (`scripts/deploy-smoke.ts`, [decision 0250](../../docs/knowledge/decisions/0250-a-smoke-check-asks-the-live-worker-for-drawn-pictures-after-.md)).

## Layout

- `src/templates.ts` the card types (`site`, `page`, `profile`, `post`, `plot`, `near`, `look`) and their element trees. `src/plot.ts` the plot photo's drawing (issue #34): our own SVG of a plot from data the server builds with the sim's palette, paths and flooring from their marks (`PlotPaving`), decor as itself (`PLOT_DECOR`, Halloween's bat bunting, cauldron, and candy bowl included, RFC 0022, winter's snowman, string of lights, little fir, and sled, RFC 0017, in the colors of `WINTER_HEX` in `packages/ui/src/looks.ts`, Thanksgiving's cornucopia and leaf garland, RFC 0034, as `THANKSGIVING_HEX` and `LEAF_POINTS` there have them, and Hanukkah's menorah, its candles unlit since photos are daylight, and dreidel, as `HANUKKAH_HEX` has them), furniture as itself (`PLOT_FURNITURE`, RFC 0016), ponds as water with a stone bank only where no pond runs on and a lily pad on some tiles, as the map draws them (`pondSvg`, RFC 0023), and what grows in its planters (`PlotCrop`: a sprout whose fruit set part grown, or a crop on a vine like a pumpkin (`vine`, which the server reads from the sim's catalog), by how far along it is, as the map paints them); colors are checked by `safeColor`, every number is kept to its tile, and the owner's name is drawn as text by the template. A photo carries the moment it shows (`sky`, decision 0300) and a `stamp` under its caption, the day, the season, the time of day and the weather in the server's own words. A crop sets its fruit at `FRUIT_SET`, well before it's ready, swelling from a green bud to the crop's own color, so a garden reads as what's in it; the same two numbers are in the map's `paintCrop` (`packages/client/src/render/blocks.ts`), and they move together. A named plot (`title`, decision 0121) wears its name as the title, with the owner's home under it. The card is a postcard two ways (issue #80): a photo taken to post (`posted`, what `POST /v1/plots/photo` draws) is a tall instant photo in the middle with its caption and the brand on the frame, since a post's card crops it to a square from the middle, so nothing that matters may leave the middle 630 pixels; the card for a link puts the photo on one side and its words, its stamp and the brand on the other, so no band of paper is left empty. The owner's pet comes as the sim's own shapes (`PlotPet`), and `petSvg` draws only the tags, attributes, and path data it checks, never a name ([RFC 0019](../../docs/rfcs/0019-pets.md)). `src/h.ts` the element builder. `src/text.ts` cleaning, clipping, counts and dates.
- Pictures by link ([decision 0160](../../docs/knowledge/decisions/0160-pictures-by-link-are-public-cached-pngs-of-a-plot-a-look-and.md)): `plotSvg` draws its plot through `areaParts`, any rectangle of the world (`PlotArea`: ground, tints, ponds, blocks, crops, hearths (each with a chimney and its smoke held still over it, decision 0304), pets, and figures back to front), which `src/pictures.ts` reuses for the map around someone (`nearSvg`, which is one `areaParts` call with a `ring` under them and the `sky` over them) and beside the look card (`lookSvg`: the figure on its theme's ground with the pet beside it). A plot photo can carry `figures` too, drawn faded when `faded` says flooring is over them. An area's `floors` (RFC 0028) are the floors above the ground floor, each a list of flooring (`PlotFlooring`) and blocks drawn after the hearths, over the floors under it, with a shadow that falls 0.22 of a tile further per floor, as the map draws them; with `floorPlan`, the top one is a floor plan, and every tile where it has no flooring is dimmed first. Which floors come is the server's choice: all of them from above, or those up to the one a floor plan shows. Figures are `Drawing`s (`src/drawing.ts`): path data, colors, clips, and pattern tiles recorded from the map's figure by `packages/ui/src/figure-svg.ts`, each part checked by `drawingSvg` (paths of commands and numbers, `safeColor`, its own clip and tile indices, capped counts), with ids prefixed so several share one SVG. `src/color.ts` holds `safeColor`.
- `src/sky.ts` the sky every picture from above shares ([decision 0300](../../docs/knowledge/decisions/0300-a-plot-photo-shows-the-moment-it-was-taken-in-one-shared-sky.md)): `Sky` (how dark it is and the weather), `skyParts` (the weather held still, then the map's own light: a warm glaze at dawn and dusk, the night as a `mix-blend-mode: multiply` that keeps every hue under a thin violet glaze, and hearths glowing after dark), and `nightWash`, the same night as a flat wash for the one thing a card draws over the plot rather than in it, the owner's picture of their home. resvg honours `mix-blend-mode` in the CSS form only (`style="..."`), never as an attribute.
- `src/render.ts` `createCards()`: boots the wasm once, rasterizes the paper, renders a card to PNG.
- `src/node.ts` and `src/worker.ts` the two engines, and `src/fonts.ts` the font files for Node. `src/index.ts` the types and pure helpers, with no wasm. `cloudflare/worker.ts` wraps `src/worker.ts`'s renderer as the cards Worker's one RPC method; keep its `createCards` boot once per isolate, since resvg refuses a second `initWasm`.
- `src/images.ts` reads a picture's type and size from its header (`probeImage`); `imageDataUri()` refuses formats resvg can't draw and pictures too big to decode in a Worker's memory. `probeImage` is the one header reader in the repo: the server sizes uploads with it (`packages/server/src/image-size.ts`), so it reads hostile bytes and follows the server's rule for them. It checks every length against the file, allocates nothing from a length it read, and walks a JPEG once. A change to what it accepts changes what size a post records, so it comes with a case in `cards.test.ts` and has to pass `packages/server/src/image-size.test.ts`.
- `src/brand.ts` palette, mark and grain, generated by `pnpm brand` from `scripts/brand/logo.ts`. Don't edit it.

## Looking at them

`pnpm --filter @terrakin/cards samples <dir>` writes every sample card (`src/samples.ts`) as `og-<name>.png`. Look at them after any template change.
