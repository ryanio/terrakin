# packages/cards/

Link preview cards (the 1200x630 images X, Discord, Slack and iMessage show for a shared link). satori lays out a template as SVG, resvg (wasm) draws the PNG. The same code runs in the Cloudflare Worker and in Node. [Decision 0028](../../docs/knowledge/decisions/0028-link-preview-cards-and-page-meta-at-the-edge.md) says why.

Depends on nothing in the repo. The server turns API data into a `Card` (`packages/server/src/og.ts`) and serves it.

## Rules

- **Resident text is drawn, never parsed.** Names, bios and posts are string children of `text()` boxes. Nothing here builds markup from them; `svg()` and `img()` only ever take our own markup and data URIs the server checked with `imageDataUri()`.
- **Clean and cut every string a resident wrote.** `drawable()` drops what the fonts can't draw (emoji, scripts outside Latin) and returns undefined when too little is left; `clip()` caps the length. satori wraps text but never shortens it.
- **Keep a render cheap**, about 100 ms in Node. No blurred `boxShadow` (each is a Gaussian blur), no SVG filters per card. The paper grain is rasterized once per process (`render.ts`).
- **Bump `CARDS_VERSION`** in `templates.ts` when a card's look changes, so cached cards are drawn again.
- **Fonts are the static WOFF files from @fontsource** (satori can't read WOFF2). `fonts.ts` lists them for Node and `worker.ts` imports the same files for wrangler; a test keeps the two in step.
- **satori stays pinned at 0.32.0.** Later versions load HarfBuzz from a separate wasm at run time, which Workers refuse. A test fails on a satori that imports it, and `.github/dependabot.yml` ignores satori, so move the pin by hand and only after a card draws under `pnpm cf:dev`. After each deploy, CI's `smoke` job asks the live Worker for three pictures and fails on the redirect to `/og.png` a Worker that can't draw sends (`scripts/deploy-smoke.ts`, [decision 0250](../../docs/knowledge/decisions/0250-a-smoke-check-asks-the-live-worker-for-drawn-pictures-after-.md)).

## Layout

- `src/templates.ts` the card types (`site`, `page`, `profile`, `post`, `plot`, `near`, `look`) and their element trees. `src/plot.ts` the plot photo's drawing (issue #34): our own SVG of a plot from data the server builds with the sim's palette, paths and floors from their marks (`PlotPaving`), decor as itself (`PLOT_DECOR`, Halloween's bat bunting, cauldron, and candy bowl included, RFC 0022, and winter's snowman, string of lights, little fir, and sled, RFC 0017, in the colors of `WINTER_HEX` in `packages/ui/src/looks.ts`), furniture as itself (`PLOT_FURNITURE`, RFC 0016), ponds as water with a stone bank only where no pond runs on and a lily pad on some tiles, as the map draws them (`pondSvg`, RFC 0023), and what grows in its planters (`PlotCrop`: a sprout with its fruit once ripe, or a crop on a vine like a pumpkin (`vine`, which the server reads from the sim's catalog), by how far along it is, as the map paints them); colors are checked by `safeColor`, every number is kept to its tile, and the owner's name is drawn as text by the template. A named plot (`title`, decision 0121) wears its name as the title, with the owner's home and its place under it. A photo taken to post (`posted`, what `POST /v1/plots/photo` draws) has no card border and a smaller frame with the brand at its foot, so a post's card frames it once and a phone's crop of its sides cuts nothing; the card for a link keeps the border and the corner signature. The owner's pet comes as the sim's own shapes (`PlotPet`), and `petSvg` draws only the tags, attributes, and path data it checks, never a name ([RFC 0019](../../docs/rfcs/0019-pets.md)). `src/h.ts` the element builder. `src/text.ts` cleaning, clipping, counts and dates.
- Pictures by link ([decision 0160](../../docs/knowledge/decisions/0160-pictures-by-link-are-public-cached-pngs-of-a-plot-a-look-and.md)): `plotSvg` draws its plot through `areaParts`, any rectangle of the world (`PlotArea`: ground, tints, ponds, blocks, crops, hearths, pets, and figures back to front), which `src/pictures.ts` reuses for the map around someone (`nearSvg`, with a ring under them, the weather as a wash with rain or snow at fixed spots, and the map's light with hearths glowing after dark) and beside the look card (`lookSvg`: the figure on its theme's ground with the pet beside it). A plot photo can carry `figures` too, drawn faded when `faded` says a floor is over them. An area's `storeys` (RFC 0028) are the storeys above the ground floor, each a list of floors (`PlotFloor`) and blocks drawn after the hearths, over the storeys under it, with a shadow that falls 0.22 of a tile further per storey, as the map draws them; with `floorPlan`, the top one is a floor plan, and every tile where it has no floor is dimmed first. Which storeys come is the server's choice: all of them from above, or those up to the one a floor plan shows. Figures are `Drawing`s (`src/drawing.ts`): path data, colors, clips, and pattern tiles recorded from the map's figure by `packages/ui/src/figure-svg.ts`, each part checked by `drawingSvg` (paths of commands and numbers, `safeColor`, its own clip and tile indices, capped counts), with ids prefixed so several share one SVG. `src/color.ts` holds `safeColor`.
- `src/render.ts` `createCards()`: boots the wasm once, rasterizes the paper, renders a card to PNG.
- `src/node.ts` and `src/worker.ts` the two engines, and `src/fonts.ts` the font files for Node. `src/index.ts` the types and pure helpers, with no wasm.
- `src/images.ts` reads a picture's type and size from its header; `imageDataUri()` refuses formats resvg can't draw and pictures too big to decode in a Worker's memory.
- `src/brand.ts` palette, mark and grain, generated by `pnpm brand` from `scripts/brand/logo.ts`. Don't edit it.

## Looking at them

`pnpm --filter @terrakin/cards samples <dir>` writes every sample card (`src/samples.ts`) as `og-<name>.png`. Look at them after any template change.
