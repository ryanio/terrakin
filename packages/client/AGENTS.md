# packages/client/

Mobile-first web client. It shows what the server says and never decides anything. How the pages fit together: [docs/architecture.md](../../docs/architecture.md).

## Rules

- **No game rules.** The server decides; show its answer, in plain words (`worldProblem` in `src/things.ts` for the world's refusals). You may hold back a button the mirror or your inventory already shows it would refuse (no plot for Build, an unripe crop), as long as the reason is on screen and the check is the sim's own exported function (`canBuildOn`, `isReady`, `harvestFits`), never a copy ([decision 0052](../../docs/knowledge/decisions/0052-the-client-holds-back-actions-the-sim-would-refuse-using-the.md)). UI hints like the reach outline are fine.
- **Mirror, don't simulate.** `src/mirror.ts` applies server events. If `apply()` reports a gap, reload the snapshot.
- **Resident text is text.** Build DOM with `h()` from `@terrakin/ui/dom` or `textContent`. Never `innerHTML`, `insertAdjacentHTML`, or template strings for names, chat, posts, or notes.
- **Phone first.** Test at 390x844 before desktop. Tap targets at least 44px (`--tap` is 52px), inputs at 16px so iOS doesn't zoom, respect `env(safe-area-inset-*)`.
- **Only our media.** An img, video, avatar, or model loader takes a URL only through `isMediaUrl` (`/media/m_` plus 16 hex). Letter pictures go through `isLetterMediaUrl` (`src/letter-media.ts`), fetched with the token and shown from a `blob:` URL the page revokes.
- **The first load has a budget.** The build fails when the entry script and what it imports up front pass `FIRST_LOAD_BUDGET` gzipped (`packages/client/vite.config.ts`, [decision 0110](../../docs/knowledge/decisions/0110-the-first-page-load-has-a-gzipped-size-budget-the-build-enfo.md)). Code only some visits need goes behind `import()`: every page but the feed is built through `lazyView` in `main.ts`'s `pageFor`, so a new page goes there as an `import()` too. The build drops the protocol schemas' literal descriptions (`noSchemaDescriptions`, `src/strip-describe.ts`, [decision 0163](../../docs/knowledge/decisions/0163-the-client-build-drops-the-protocol-schemas-descriptions.md)), since the app never reads one, and gives zod stand-ins for its JSON Schema modules (`noJsonSchema`, `src/no-json-schema.ts`), since the app never asks for JSON Schema; avatar figures load beside the first load ([decision 0231](../../docs/knowledge/decisions/0231-the-client-build-leaves-out-zod-s-json-schema-code-and-draws.md)). A helper a first-load module needs lives in a small module of its own, never in a page's module (`letter-media.ts`, `visit-plot.ts`).
- **three.js stays lazy.** Import `three` only in `src/model-viewer.ts` and `src/scene3d/`, reached through `import()` ([decision 0013](../../docs/knowledge/decisions/0013-load-three-js-only-when-someone-opens-a-3d-model.md)). `scene3d.test.ts` fails otherwise. 3D work follows the art direction and budget in [decision 0030](../../docs/knowledge/decisions/0030-3d-art-direction-and-performance-budget.md), and the world in 3D the view radius and frame budget in [decision 0060](../../docs/knowledge/decisions/0060-the-world-in-3d-draws-a-view-radius-around-you-within-a-fram.md).
- **Sound stays lazy, and off until tapped.** The sound code (`src/sound/soundscape.ts`, `voices.ts`, `mix.ts`) is reached only through `import()` from `src/sound/switch.ts`, which makes the AudioContext inside the tap that turns sound on, never before ([decision 0097](../../docs/knowledge/decisions/0097-the-world-s-sound-is-made-with-code-off-until-the-speaker-is.md)). `sound.test.ts` fails on a static import, and `e2e/sound.spec.ts` on a context made before the tap. Sounds are made with code, never files, and only your own moments make one.
- **Telemetry gets templates, not people.** GA4 and Sentry never see names, text, tokens, or ids: pages are templated (`/r/:id`), titles fixed, clicks reduced to `tag#id.class`. They run only on a production build served from terrakin.org, and Sentry only in browsers new enough to run the app. What each may receive is [decision 0015](../../docs/knowledge/decisions/0015-ga4-and-sentry-and-what-they-may-receive.md); `telemetry.test.ts` pins it. Our own breadcrumbs go through `appCrumb` and carry codes and templates, never text. In GA4 admin, Enhanced measurement's "Page changes based on browser history events" and "Form interactions" must stay off.
- **The CSP is in `vite.config.ts`.** A new third-party script, font, or endpoint needs adding there, or the browser blocks it. The docs are static pages and load nothing at all beyond the site's stylesheet and fonts.
- **A new route needs a case in `matchPage`** (`packages/server/src/page-meta.ts`).
- **The e2e suite is the contract.** Changing element ids, controls, or the join flow means updating `e2e/` in the same commit ([e2e/AGENTS.md](../../e2e/AGENTS.md)).
- **Keep it light.** Plain DOM and canvas. A UI framework needs a decision record.
- **Pick a page layout.** Every page is a `pageLayout` (main and sidebar: the feed, profiles, posts, the Town Hall, shop, market, bounties, games and a game's table, your things, the purse, the collection book), a `wideLayout` (a grid of cards: Visit, Galleries), or a `.column` (forms and lists: notifications, letters, people, invites, claims). Put what someone came for in the main column and what supports it in the sidebar ([decision 0191](../../docs/knowledge/decisions/0191-every-page-is-a-sidebar-layout-a-wide-card-grid-or-a-column.md)).
- **Build from the design system.** Tokens are on `:root` in `packages/ui/src/tokens.css`, sizes come from its scales, and layout from the primitives (`stack`, `cluster`, `plain-list`) in the class list ([packages/ui/AGENTS.md](../ui/AGENTS.md)); shared pieces (`.column`, `.paper`, `.card`, `.pill`, `.btn-primary`, `.avatar`, ...) are in `packages/ui/src/base.css`, which `src/style.css` imports. The staff app (`packages/admin/`) uses the same two files.
- **Use the shared components.** Root rule 11. Avatars, a resident as a link (`personLink`), badges, times (`timeAgo`), state cards, empty notes, checkbox rows, sheets, and profile and post paths come from `packages/ui/` ([packages/ui/AGENTS.md](../ui/AGENTS.md)), with their styles in `base.css`. Also `actProblem` (`src/api.ts`) for action results, `actFromButton` (`src/act.ts`) for a button that sends one action, says how it went, and refreshes the purse and the page (the shop, the market, and the bounties), and `colorChips`, `hairColorChips`, `hairChips`, and `shapeChips` (`src/join-form.ts`). Don't build one by hand or restyle it here; `src/shared-components.test.ts` fails if you do, and on a selector defined twice in `src/style.css`.

## Writing tests

Before adding a unit test here, answer four questions, and hold the test until you can:

1. What behavior does it protect: something a person sees, a contract, or a rule above?
2. What regression makes it fail?
3. Why doesn't an existing test catch that already? Extend a table or a shared fixture instead of adding a near-copy.
4. Does it need an export, flag, or hook that no production code uses? Then test the real caller instead.

Test at the lowest level that catches the bug, as the root `AGENTS.md` says: a game rule in `packages/sim/`, a wire format in `packages/protocol/`, routing and auth in `packages/server/`, a journey in `e2e/`. Here that means the pure functions (words, layouts, the mirror, the walker) given real inputs. A test that copies a constant or a list, restates a lookup table, or checks a value against the function that made it protects nothing. The source checks in `shared-components.test.ts`, `scene3d.test.ts`, and `sound.test.ts` stay, because each is the cheapest guard of a rule above.

In 3D a storey is one wall tall (`STOREY_HEIGHT` and `storeyY` in `scene3d/layout.ts`): a block upstairs is one more instance of its kind's mesh, lifted by its storey, and a floor upstairs is its kind's look (`groundTiles`) on a timber slab, every slab of a plot in one instanced mesh (`floorSlabs` in `scene3d/paths.ts`), so a loft adds one draw plus any kind the ground floor lacks. Stairs are a flight of treads a storey tall, turned to climb from an open tile (`stairsTurn`). The world in 3D cuts away the plot you stand on at your storey and shows every other plot whole (`readChunk`'s `cut`), figures stand on their storey, and the camera and the reach outline rise with you. In both 3D views someone on a storey the cut leaves out is faded, their name tag still drawn (`fadeFigure` in `scene3d/figure.ts`), as the map fades someone under a floor. The 3D home view (`/r/:id/3d`) draws every storey (`plotLayout`'s `top` is the plot's highest), with a picker under the bar on a home with a loft, "Ground floor" and "Upstairs" (`storeyChips`), that starts on your storey while you stand on the plot (`startStorey`) and else on the whole home; picking one builds the plot's blocks, floors, and slabs again with what's above it cut away (`cutAway`), neighbors whole, and darkens the tops of the walls left open (`cutWalls`, caps in the slab mesh). A photo frames the whole home with its top storey (`homeFrame`), whichever storey is picked.

## Running

`pnpm dev` from the root starts the server and Vite. Vite proxies `/v1` (including the socket), `/media`, the Markdown twins, the sitemaps, and `/og/` to `TERRAKIN_SERVER` (default `http://localhost:8787`). Open the printed network URL on your phone to test on a real device.
