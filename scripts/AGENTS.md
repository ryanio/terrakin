# scripts/

Repo tooling. Plain Node with type stripping, so it runs right after `pnpm install` with no build step.

| Script | Command | What |
|--------|---------|------|
| `gen.ts` | `pnpm gen`, `pnpm gen:check` | Writes everything derived from the route table and site config: the API blocks in `SKILL.md` and `llms.txt`, `openapi.json`, discovery files, docs guides, `docs/site/lastmod.json`, everything built from `CHANGELOG.md` (with the API fingerprint check), and everything built from the posts in `docs/devlog`. The file header lists every output. |
| `kb.ts` | `pnpm kb`, `pnpm kb new <kind> "Title"` | Rebuilds `docs/knowledge/INDEX.md` and creates decision, learning, and handoff entries. A new decision is numbered past the newest on `origin/main`, and `--check` fails when two decisions or two RFCs share a number. |
| `economy-sim.ts` | `node scripts/economy-sim.ts [--start 2024-10-04] [--no-shop] [--no-shop-counter] [--shop-trips 100] [--no-fishing] [--no-recipes] [--picks sell] [--no-holidays] [--holiday-prices before] [--no-floors] [--no-levels] [--no-jobs] [--townsfolk-spend 80] [--starter-gift 0] [--starter-from resident] [--job-pay 0] [--job-pay-from treasury] [--set key=value]` | Plays a month of residents, their gardens, and the town shop through the real sim, and prints supply per active resident, what the town paid, and what residents spent. The default month is in autumn, so it has autumn's stock and buying in it; `--start` plays another season (`--start 2024-12-04` is a winter month). Some residents fish (RFC 0023), from their own PRNG stream, so `--no-fishing` plays the month exactly as it was before fishing. A season's wishes are skipped in other seasons without a draw, so adding one leaves the other seasons' months as they were. Recipes are learned by default (RFC 0024): free picks, cards, lessons from neighbors and townsfolk, and recipe pages, from their own PRNG stream, so `--no-recipes` plays the month as it was before them; it also prints how many days newcomers take to a first sale and to knowing what they sell. Residents keep Halloween by default (RFC 0022): some want a costume and a piece of its decor and buy them while it runs, from their own PRNG stream, so `--no-holidays` plays the month as it was before; the world logs `lower_holiday_prices` as terrakin.org does, and `--holiday-prices before` plays decision 0107's prices. It prints what newcomers had to spend in their first week, who bought a costume and decor, and what holiday stock burned. Half the regulars build up by default (RFC 0028): they save for `add_floor`, skipping wishes that would eat into it, pick up branches within reach, and put up stairs and plank a loft, from their own PRNG stream, so `--no-floors` plays the month as it was before them; it prints RFC 0028's five checks (supply per active resident each week, when builders hold the price and add a floor, newcomers' first week at the shop, how long the wood takes against the anglers' rods, and what floors burn against the mint). Residents play for levels by default (RFC 0029): the world logs `open_levels`, `own_plot_pickups`, and `open_bounties`, and from their own PRNG stream some residents pick up finds, make furniture from wood and stone near home, keep casting until Foraging's cap, hold a plot event a week and post a bounty a week, and sit down to rated games, while a quarter of the regulars play one skill in earnest. `--no-levels` draws none of it and plays the month as it was before. It prints RFC 0029's six checks: the first day, a regular's first weeks, the month-end level against every cap every day, the days each skill takes to level 5, a household and a ring of ten played in a small world of their own, and supply against the same month run again with `--no-levels`. Residents take the town's jobs by default (RFC 0030): the world logs `open_jobs`, and from their own PRNG stream about half the regulars take a job when the board has one in a skill they play and they have what it takes, a few take any job they could do, and everyone playing one skill in earnest takes its jobs. They make a job's thing before the day's selling, pick up its find, cast on for its fish, and hold its lesson, event, or game after the day's own; one who takes any job buys the seed a harvest job asks for. `--no-jobs` draws none of it and plays the month as it was before. It prints RFC 0030's eight checks: the bounds, in the month and for two accounts in a small world of their own, one playing every day and one three days a week; a regular's jobs a week, and their level against the same resident in the month run again with `--no-jobs`; every cap and every job against the median regular; the days each skill takes to level 5 with its jobs; a household, a jar passed round it, and a ring of ten; supply and what the town paid; the board over the month; and how often each job is finished. It ends with issue #63's coin flow: the treasury's lowest and month-end balance, how long welcome gifts waited, and the median month-end purse. Four flags play coin flow that doesn't exist yet, and change nothing when left at their defaults: `--townsfolk-spend` is the percent of their budgets the townsfolk hand out (80; terrakin.org hands out about 10), `--starter-gift 15` adds a welcomer who gives each newcomer a starter gift from their own purse and reports how long those waited (`--starter-from town` makes it part of the welcome gift instead, and `townsfolk` part of the townsfolk welcome tip), and `--job-pay 5` pays for each finished job from the treasury while it keeps its reserve (`--job-pay-from mint` mints it). Their choices are `coin-flow.ts`, pure and tested ([decision 0278](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0278-coin-flow-the-townsfolk-welcome-tip-rises-to-25-and-jobs-sti.md)). It also plays a circle (RFC 0033) in small worlds of its own beside the month, so the month is unchanged: a circle of 6 meeting weekly against the same people at an ordinary event (Hosting points and karma a member), and a circle of 12 reacting to mates first and as a ring of every member reacting to every mate every day (appreciation coins and the median member's purse) ([decision 0279](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0279-kindreds-land-in-the-sim-with-rfc-0033-s-answers-weeks-from-.md)). The shop is a place you go to by default ([RFC 0042](../docs/rfcs/0042-actions-that-need-you-there.md)): the world logs `shop_in_person` as terrakin.org does, and a resident walks to the counter over the sim's own route and comes home for every purchase and sale, so the run models the shop the live world has. `--no-shop-counter` serves from anywhere, as before the rule. The walk costs no coins and no time in the sim, so the only channel the rule has on the numbers is whether a trade happens at all, and `--shop-trips` is that one assumption, out in the open: the percent of days a resident who wants the shop makes the walk (100 by default, the designed behaviour). Measured over the default month, the counter suppresses buying (down 17%) more than selling (down 5%), because buying is a wish list and selling is what a garden produced, so the net burn shrinks: residents end the month richer (supply per active resident 274 to 302) and the treasury thins (3744 to 3633), which is the direction RFC 0042's own read bracketed. Residents no longer plant on the column south of their hearth, since the starter hut's doorway is on it and they were walling themselves in; that alone moves a pre-counter run about 0.8%. Rerun it before changing a number in `ECONOMY`, the pantry, the shop, a season, `RECIPES_RULES`, `FLOORS` (`--set floors.price=150`), `PROGRESS` (`--set progress.step=25`), or `JOBS` (`--set jobs.points=15`) ([decisions 0039](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0039-phase-1-coin-numbers-tuned-with-a-simulated-month.md), [0052](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0052-the-town-shop-sells-decor-and-wear-buys-a-rotating-few-goods.md), [0079](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0079-autumn-s-numbers-pumpkins-pumpkin-pie-and-soup-hay-bales-and.md), [0124](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0124-winter-s-numbers-cranberries-hot-cranberry-punch-winter-deco.md), [0186](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0186-recipes-keep-the-rfc-s-card-prices-and-page-rate-the-economy.md), [0210](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0210-halloween-s-costumes-and-decor-cost-half-from-a-logged-lower.md), [0242](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0242-a-storey-costs-200-coins-the-economy-run-with-builders-says-.md), [0246](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0246-level-n-to-n-1-takes-20-points-times-n-a-lesson-and-a-counte.md), and [0255](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0255-a-job-pays-10-points-and-three-count-a-week-the-economy-run-.md)). |

| `deploy.ts` | `pnpm cf:deploy` | Runs `wrangler deploy` only from the tip of `origin/main` with a clean tree, the cards Worker first and the main one after ([decision 0299](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0299-card-drawing-moves-to-a-worker-of-its-own-reached-over-a-ser.md): a service binding to a Worker that doesn't exist is refused). An older Worker can't replay a log the newer one wrote, so a stale deploy takes the world down ([docs/deploy.md](../docs/deploy.md)). | `deploy-smoke.ts` | `node scripts/deploy-smoke.ts [--base https://terrakin.org] [--wait 120] [--deployed] [--version <id>] [--version-wait 600] [--script /assets/index-abc.js] [--seed text]` (CI's `smoke` job) | Asks the live Worker for what the suite can't see from Node ([decision 0250](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0250-a-smoke-check-asks-the-live-worker-for-drawn-pictures-after-.md)): `/v1/health`, `/v1/world` with every switch `packages/server/cloudflare/worker.ts` sets that the snapshot shows (`SNAPSHOT_FIELDS`; a test fails on a switch with no row), a plot, a look, and the map around someone as drawn PNGs and never the redirect to `/og.png`, the types of `/skill.md`, `/llms.txt`, and `/changelog.xml`, and the homepage's hashed script. About ten GETs, no token, nothing written. `--version` waits, asking `/v1/health` every 30 seconds for up to `--version-wait` seconds, until the world's `build` and the Worker's `terrakin-worker-build` name that deploy, and only then asks for the world, the skill file (which must then be this checkout's), and the pictures; when the world never gets there it fails `build` and says what it didn't ask ([decision 0263](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0263-the-smoke-check-waits-for-the-world-object-to-run-the-deploy.md)). `--wait` asks again for what failed every 30 seconds for that many seconds, and a picture at most 3 times in a run (`PICTURE_TRIES`). Each picture's line says from its `Cache-Status` whether the Worker drew it now; when none of the three was, up to `EXTRA_PICTURES` more maps are asked for once each, so a run causes at most 12 draws, the per-address cap. A run with no fresh draw passes and says it couldn't prove one. Without `--version`, `--deployed` adds whether the live skill file is this checkout's and never fails. Exits 0 when every check passed, 1 when one didn't, 2 when it couldn't start (an empty `--version` among them). What each check means is in [docs/deploy.md](../docs/deploy.md#the-smoke-check-after-a-deploy). |
| `e2e-needed.ts` | `node scripts/e2e-needed.ts` (CI's `changes` job) | Prints `e2e=true` or `e2e=false`: whether this CI run needs e2e. A sim-only push skipping e2e is a trade-off: its unit tests own the rules, and the nightly 2D run catches a sim change that breaks a spec. ## Rules

- `deploy-smoke.ts` only reads. A check that needs a token, a resident, or a write doesn't go in it, and a new picture check keeps the run under the draw caps in [packages/server/AGENTS.md](../packages/server/AGENTS.md).
- A script that reaches a workspace package through a plain `import` runs as `node --import ./scripts/resolve-ts.mjs <script>` (the three `townsfolk` ones do), since the packages import each other without file extensions and Node resolves a script's whole import graph before it runs a line of it. A script that reaches them with `await import(...)` registers the same hook itself, as `gen.ts` and `economy-sim.ts` do.
- Generators are deterministic: rerunning with no input change writes identical files. Check mode compares content or hashes, never dates.
- Never hand-edit generated output. Change the source and rerun.
- `pnpm townsfolk` against `https://terrakin.org` writes to production. Do a `--dry-run` first, and treat it as the owner's call.
- `pnpm townsfolk -- --handles` is a dry run unless you pass `--send`, which claims each persona's handle (`handle-plan.ts` decides, pure and tested).
- `pnpm townsfolk -- --routines --pets --roofs` is a dry run that prints each request it would make, unless you pass `--send`, which sets each persona's routines, adopts its pet, and puts its roof up (`routine-plan.ts`, `pet-plan.ts`, and `roof-plan.ts` decide, pure and tested). It never adopts a second pet. Every roof is covered in a free block, so it takes nothing and gives the old covering back ([decision 0330](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0330-the-townsfolk-s-homes-wear-roofs-in-the-free-coverings-and-n.md)). With `--send` against `https://terrakin.org` it changes the live world: the owner's call.
- `pnpm townsfolk:avatars` is a dry run unless you pass `--send`, which uploads each picked portrait and sets it as that persona's avatar (`avatar-plan.ts` decides, pure and tested). With `--send` against `https://terrakin.org` it changes production profiles: the owner's call.
- `pnpm townsfolk:tips` is a dry run unless you pass `--send`. With `--send` against `https://terrakin.org` it gives real coins: the owner's call. Its choosing logic lives in `packages/server/src/tip-plan.ts`, pure and tested with fixtures, and shared with the server's daily run (`packages/server/src/townsfolk-tips.ts`), which gives the tips on terrakin.org; keep the network and files in `tips.ts`.
- Each persona's `specialties`, the recipes the server's lessons run has it teach (RFC 0024), live in `packages/server/src/lesson-plan.ts`, which `personas.ts` reads like `TIP_NOTES`. Nothing here sends them.
