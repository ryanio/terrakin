# sim/

The rules engine. If a rule decides what's allowed in the world, it lives here and nowhere else.

## Invariants (tests enforce these; don't weaken them)

- **Pure and deterministic.** No `Date`, `Math.random`, timers, network, filesystem, or host globals. The tsconfig has no DOM or Node types on purpose. If you need randomness, add a seeded RNG to `WorldState` and draw from it.
- **Check, then commit.** `prepare()` runs every check and mutates nothing; its `commit()` makes the change. `apply()` is both in one call. A rejected input leaves state byte-identical (`hashWorld` before == after). The server persists between the two steps.
- **`seq` goes up by exactly one per accepted input.**
- **Events describe every change.** Clients mirror the world from events alone, without re-running rules. If you change state, emit an event for it.
- **State is plain JSON data.** Records, arrays, numbers, strings. No `Map`, classes, or `undefined` values, so `canonicalJson`/`hashWorld`/`cloneWorld` stay exact.
- **Replay must reproduce.** An accepted log replays to the same hash forever. Changing a rule that would change how existing logs replay needs an RFC ([decision 0003](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0003-deterministic-sim-with-input-log.md)) and a bump of `REPLAY_VERSION` (`src/replay.ts`), since the server boots from snapshots and no longer re-runs old inputs ([decision 0070](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0070-replay-version-marks-rule-changes-that-make-old-snapshots-un.md)).
- **A snapshot plus the rest of the log is a straight replay.** A world written with `JSON.stringify` and read back with `JSON.parse` must apply the inputs after it to the same hash and the same event bytes. `replay.test.ts` checks every split point of every log in `src/fixtures/`, so a new fixture is covered by adding the file. Rules may not depend on anything outside the state.
- **Ids from inputs are own keys.** Look a record up by an id from a command with `own(record, id)` or `residentById(state, id)` (`src/own.ts`), never `record[id]` or `id in record`: `__proto__`, `constructor`, or `toString` would find what every object inherits, and since inputs are logged, the damage would replay on every restart. Write under such an id only after it was found, or after `isOwnableKey`. `own.test.ts` throws those ids at every input that takes one.
- **New state is absent until used.** A new field stays off old records until an input sets it, and bookkeeping without an event starts only after a new input (like the first `new_day`), so old logs keep their hash. `src/fixtures/pre-town-log.ts` pins one such hash.

## Where things are

- `src/types.ts` world, command, event, and rejection types. Start here.
- `src/apply.ts` the rules. One `case` per command.
- `src/world.ts` geometry helpers and `DEFAULT_CONFIG`.
- `src/walk.ts` walking ([decision 0072](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0072-walking-is-eight-ways-around-solid-buildings-paced-by-your-f.md)): `stepFrom`, the one rule for a step (eight directions, never off the world, into a block, or onto a solid building, and never diagonally past a corner), checked for every `move` and putter step; `groundOf` and `worldGround`, the ground a step reads, which the client builds from its mirror with the same function; `walkTree` and `route`, breadth-first walks over the same rule, for the putter planner and the client's tap-to-walk; and the server-only `solid_buildings` (`checkSolidBuildings`, from `TOWN_ACTOR`), which sets `state.solidBuildings` and steps anyone standing on the Town Hall or the shop off to the nearest open tile. Before it residents walked across both, so older logs replay as they were made. `src/fixtures/walk-log.ts` pins a log with diagonal steps and the switch.
- `src/hash.ts` canonical JSON + FNV-1a fingerprint. `src/keys.ts` the `"x,y"` record keys for tiles and plots.
- `src/replay.ts` rebuild state from a log, and `REPLAY_VERSION`, which the server stores with each world snapshot (RFC 0014).
- `src/presence.ts` presence that comes with acting (RFC 0014 step 4, [decision 0071](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0071-presence-comes-with-acting-once-implicit-presence-is-logged.md)): `implicit_presence` turns it on (`state.implicitPresence`, absent until then) and `leave_idle {ids}` takes everyone in `ids` offline with a `left` each, both from `TOWN_ACTOR`. Once it's on, `prepare` brings a known, offline resident back for their own command (not `join` or `leave`) with `join`'s rules (`rejoined` in `apply.ts`), checking with them back in place and putting everything back before it returns; `commit` emits `joined` first. `asJoined` is the same as a read-only view, for planning walks and dry runs. `src/fixtures/presence-log.ts` pins a log that uses it.
- `src/routines.ts` offline routines ([RFC 0009](../docs/rfcs/0009-offline-routines.md), [decision 0082](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0082-routines-are-logged-steps-the-sim-checks-due-from-their-utc-.md)): `set_routines` (a resident's list in `state.routines`, at most one `walk_home`, `stroll`, and `greet`, in `ROUTINE_KINDS` order, absent until the first) and the server-only `routine_step {resident, routine, step}` from `TOWN_ACTOR`. A step is refused for a routine that isn't on (`not_set`), a resident who's online (`awake`), or one that used up its day (`ran_today`: `walk_home` once, a stroll `ROUTINES.strollTiles` tiles over its legs, kept in `state.routineRuns`), and then meets the resident's own checks: `home` (refused `already_home` even with the allowance due) or a putter's steps that must stay on plots they can build on. It's a server command, so it pays no allowance or pantry and never bumps `lastActiveDay`; its `moved` events carry `routine`. `planStroll` and `strollBack` are the server's planner, never replay's. `src/fixtures/routines-log.ts` pins a log that uses them.
- `src/town.ts` the Town Hall (RFC 0004): eligibility, proposals, votes, closes, builds, `TOWN_LIMITS`. A `commons_build` is `build`'s four lists at world tiles in the Commons: `COMMONS_BLOCKS` (the building blocks, decor, and furniture) and any ground, 40 changes in all, checked whole when filed (`checkPlan`) and built at close in `build`'s order with what moved since skipped (`closeBuild`), from nobody's things ([decision 0101](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0101-a-town-hall-build-lays-paths-and-places-decor-and-furniture-.md)). `checkPropose` hands its plan to `prepare`, so a dry proposal previews it. Commands the server sends itself (`new_day`, `set_townsfolk`, `close_proposal`, `void_proposal`) must come from `TOWN_ACTOR` ([decision 0026](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0026-time-enters-the-sim-as-logged-day-and-close-inputs.md)).
- `src/looks.ts` the looks catalog (RFC 0005): themes, patterns, wear and its five slots (a dress covers the bottom slot), `wearStyle`, a pattern and color per garment merged item by item ([decision 0053](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0053-any-garment-can-carry-its-own-pattern-and-color-with-bottoms.md)), and hair: `HAIR_STYLES` and `HAIR_COLORS` with their labels and colors, two look fields that `null` clears, the color kept while there's no style ([decision 0074](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0074-hair-is-a-style-and-a-color-on-the-look-drawn-under-hats-in-.md)). New slots go on the end of `WEAR_SLOTS`, so older wear lists sort the same. The protocol and client import it.
- `src/putter.ts` `planPutter`: where a `putter` walks (up to `PUTTER.steps` steps over `walkTree`, diagonals too, next to someone nearby, else a neighbor's plot or your own plot's edge, else the Commons, else anywhere open), with choices picked by a hash of the actor, `seq`, and `day`. The server calls it and logs the steps; the sim checks them like moves, so replay never runs the planner and tuning it never changes old logs. `PUTTER_MAX_STEPS`, the sim's cap on logged steps, may go up but never down ([decision 0049](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0049-putter-is-a-planned-short-walk-logged-as-its-steps-with-a-on.md)).
- `src/visit.ts` `visit {px, py}` (RFC 0020, [decision 0091](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0091-visit-is-a-jump-to-a-neighbor-s-door-logged-with-the-tile-th.md)): `visitTile`, where a visitor lands (a free tile on the plot's outermost ring, on a path if one meets the edge, with the shortest walk to its heart, `plotHeart`: its owner's hearth, else a co-owner's, else its center, so in front of a hut's doorway), which the server calls and logs with the command; and `checkVisit`, which checks the logged tile and never runs the planner, so a better planner never changes how a logged visit replays. `src/fixtures/visit-log.ts` pins a log of visits. `plotDistance` in `src/world.ts` is how far a tile is from a plot; the server's plot admires read it.
- `src/biome.ts` `biomeAt`: the ground biome of a tile, a pure function of its position. Never state, never in the log ([decision 0045](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0045-biomes-are-a-pure-function-of-position-presentation-only.md)). Gathering reads it, so changing the map changes replay and needs an RFC. `biome.test.ts` pins the map.
- `src/season.ts` `seasonOf`: the season of a world day (spring is March to May, summer June to August, autumn September to November, winter December to February, by the UTC calendar), with `seasonSpan` for where it starts and ends and `dateOfDay`/`dayOfDate` for the calendar. A pure function of `state.day` like `biomeAt` is of position: never state, never in the log. A rule that reads it changes with the calendar, so pin it in a test. The shop reads it for seasonal stock and buying (RFC 0017, below).
- `src/weather.ts` `weatherAt`: the weather of a world day and UTC hour (`clear`, `cloudy`, `rain`, `fog`, `snow`), in spells of 3 to 6 hours per day (`weatherSpells`) drawn from each season's `WEATHER_CHANCES`, snow only in winter, and `skyAt`, the weather and season at a moment on a clock, which the server and clients both use ([decision 0073](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0073-weather-is-a-pure-function-of-the-world-day-and-the-utc-hour.md)). Like `biomeAt`: never state, never in the log, and no rule reads it. `weather.test.ts` pins a known day.
- `src/palette.ts` the world's colors: ground per biome and how each season dresses it (`SEASON_GROUND`: warmer grass in autumn, snow in winter, `seasonTone` for ground outside the biomes), blocks (and how a theme dresses them), the hearth, each tile's tuft, flower, or autumn leaf (`groundTile`, which takes the season), what each path and floor looks like (`GROUND_LOOK`, shapes in tile units that the map, the build palette, both 3D views, and the plot photos all draw), and each ripe crop's color (`CROP_HEX`, its look's `body` in the catalog) with whether it grows on a vine (`onVine`) and how far along a crop is drawn (`growth`), which the map, both 3D views, the item pictures, and the plot photos share. Presentation only, like the looks catalog. `client/src/render.ts`, the 3D views, and the plot photos (`server/src/plot-photo.ts`, drawn by `cards/`) all read it, so a photo looks like the world.
- `src/economy.ts` coins (RFC 0008): `ECONOMY` (every number, tuned with `scripts/economy-sim.ts`, [decision 0039](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0039-phase-1-coin-numbers-tuned-with-a-simulated-month.md)), the treasury, purses and ledgers, the daily allowance and streak, the welcome gift, townsfolk budgets, and `give_coins` with its caps. `ownerPaired` is one owner link; `sameHousehold` is a whole household (the same resident, a linked pair, or two linked to the same one). The server-only `open_economy`, `set_owner_pairs`, `add_owner_pair`, `remove_owner_pair`, `set_maintainers`, and `daily_awards` come from `TOWN_ACTOR`.
- `src/catalog.ts` the catalog of things (RFC 0018, [decision 0084](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0084-every-kind-is-one-catalog-entry-in-one-family-the-sim-s-list.md)): one entry for every kind a resident can hold, each in one family (`FAMILIES`, a tree: food › fruit, decor › furniture, find › forest), with its role (what rules read: seed, produce, staple, resource, decor, furniture, good, piece, find), crop numbers, shop price and seasons, recipe, and look (a template and colors for pictures). The jam family recipe (`JAM`) adds `<fruit>_jam` for every kind in food › fruit when the catalog loads. Every list and table of kinds the sim has (`ITEM_KINDS`, `SEED_KINDS`, `CROPS`, `GOOD_KINDS`, `ITEM_INFO`, `CROP_INFO`, `RECIPES`, `FURNITURE_RECIPES`, the shop's item prices and `SEASON_STOCK`) and every kind type is a view of it, and the lists rules walk are frozen there by name (`STARTER_SEEDS`, `PANTRY_STAPLES`, `ROTATION_GOODS`, `ROTATION_CROPS`). See [Adding a kind](#adding-a-kind).
- `src/items.ts` growing, making, and giving (RFC 0005 step 2): the catalog's views under the names they always had (`ITEM_INFO`, `CROP_INFO`, `RECIPES`, from `catalog.ts`), `ITEMS` (every number, [decision 0051](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0051-items-open-with-a-logged-input-grow-by-the-world-s-day-and-s.md)), inventories, `plant`, `harvest`, `craft` (goods and furniture), `give` with its caps, the daily pantry (`pantryNumbers`), and placing and taking up held blocks (decor and furniture). The server-only `open_items` comes from `TOWN_ACTOR`.
- `src/gather.ts` gathering (phase 1, [decision 0063](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0063-simple-gathering-is-in-phase-1-wood-and-stone-picku.md)): `gatherableAt`, the day's fallen branches and loose stones as a pure function of tile and day, `pickupOn` for clients to draw what's still lying there, and `gather`. Changing the spawn or `GATHER`'s chances changes replay; `gather.test.ts` pins both. Only today's picked tiles live in state (`items.gathered`); `new_day` forgets them. Once the server logs `own_plot_pickups` (`checkOwnPlotPickups`, from `TOWN_ACTOR`; it sets `items.plotPickupsOwned`), only a claimed plot's owner and co-owners may gather on it (`mayGatherOn`, which clients call too); before it, anyone may, so older gathers replay. Once it logs `open_finds` (`checkOpenFinds`; it sets `items.findsOpen`, read with `findsOpen`), a tile with no branch or stone may hold a find (RFC 0021, [decision 0099](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0099-finds-spawn-by-biome-and-season-after-a-logged-open-finds-fr.md)): a second roll with its own constants walks `FIND_SPAWNS`, each find's chance per tile a day in its biome, seasonal ones only in their season. Before the switch `gatherableAt(..., false)` answers exactly as it always did, and after it branches and stones lie where they did. `FIND_SPAWNS` is frozen: a new find or new numbers is a new table behind a new switch. `src/fixtures/finds-log.ts` pins a log with gathers on both sides of the switch.
- `src/ground.ts` paths and floors (RFC 0016, [decision 0075](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0075-paths-and-floors-are-a-second-layer-that-never-blocks-and-fu.md)): `GROUND_KINDS`, `GROUND_INFO` (what one tile takes), `lay` and `lift` (`checkLay`, `checkLift`; `apply.ts` checks where, as for `place`), and `groundShort`, which clients call to hold back what they can't afford.
- `src/furniture.ts` furniture (RFC 0016): `FURNITURE_KINDS` and `FURNITURE_RECIPES`, the catalog's kinds with the role `furniture`, and `isFurnitureKind`. `craft` makes a piece at a workbench (`checkCraft` in `items.ts`); `place` and `remove` treat it like decor (`isHeldBlock`).
- `src/build.ts` `build` (RFC 0016, [decision 0076](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0076-a-build-plan-uses-plot-coordinates-refuses-whole-what-the-pl.md)): `planBuild` checks a plan against the world and works out every change without making one, `commitBuild` makes them, `checkBuild` hands back the plan with its commit so `prepare` passes it on (`Prepared.plan`, which the server answers with through `buildSummary`), and `plotPlan` reads a plot back as a plan.
- `src/display.ts` showing (RFC 0005 step 3, [decision 0059](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0059-pieces-are-made-things-from-your-own-uploads-shown-on-pedest.md)): `make_piece` (a made thing of kind `piece` from an upload), `display` and `take_down` on a `pedestal` or `frame`, `admire`, `set_gallery` (a plot's `gallery` flag), the server-only `remove_display` (staff take a thing off display, or a piece's picture everywhere), and the read helpers `displayAt`, `displaysOf`, `displayOfItem`, `heldAsideOf`, `everyGood`, and `goodById`. `display` also takes a find by its kind (RFC 0021): `items.displays` and `items.heldAside` hold a made thing (`Display`) or a find (`ShownFind`, tell them apart with `isShownFind`). A find is never admired, reported, or in a gallery, so the made-thing helpers leave finds out, and `findsOnDisplay` and `findsHeldAsideOf` read them.

Seasons (RFC 0017, [decision 0078](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0078-seasons-follow-the-utc-calendar-and-add-stock-crops-recipes-.md)): `SEASON_STOCK`, `SEASON_BUYS`, `onSale`, `stockSeason`, `buySeason`, and `seasonLastDay`. The server-only `open_shop` comes from `TOWN_ACTOR`.

The server-only `open_market` and `remove_listing` come from `TOWN_ACTOR`.

## Items

- Nothing about items runs until `open_items`, which creates `state.items`. Until then old logs replay exactly; `src/fixtures/items-log.ts` pins a log with crops, crafting, and gifts on top of the post-economy one.
- Growth reads only `state.day`: a crop stores `plantedDay` and `readyDay` and is ready once the day reaches `readyDay`. `new_day` changes nothing about crops (it resets the daily counters), so a skipped day counts like any other and there is nothing to replay per crop.
- The pantry is bookkeeping in `prepare()`'s commit, like the allowance: it pays when a resident's own input leaves them on their hearth, once a day, only when it has something to add (so it never changes state without an event), and never to townsfolk. The first one brings the starter seeds. `home` while already home is accepted when the allowance or the pantry is due.
- The first pantry gives `STARTER_SEEDS`, never every kind in `SEED_KINDS`, and every pantry tops up `PANTRY_STAPLES`, never every staple: both are frozen, so a new seed or staple (like autumn's `pumpkin_seed`) never changes what an old pantry gave. The items log's hash and `catalog.test.ts` pin them.
- `craft` takes any recipe in `GOOD_KINDS` or `FURNITURE_KINDS`, family recipes' kinds included. Asked for a family recipe's name with a kind outside its family (`tomato_jam`), it names the kinds the recipe takes.
- Seeds, produce, and staples stack as counts (absent at 0). A made thing has an id from `items.nextId`, its maker, and its made day, and keeps them wherever it goes. Every unit counts toward `inventoryMax`.
- Inventories are private, like purses. An `inventory` event belongs to its `residentId` alone; a gift makes two. `planted`, `harvested`, `gathered`, and `item_given` (no count, no note) are public. Use `inventoryOf` for `GET /v1/inventory`.
- A planter with a crop in it can't be removed. Anyone who can build on the plot may harvest it into their own inventory.
- Pieces (`PIECE_KINDS`) are made things with no recipe, so they're not in `GOOD_KINDS`; use `MADE_KINDS` and `isMadeKind` for "its own item with an id". Displays live in `items.displays`, absent until the first `display`; a displayed thing stays its displayer's (`by`), `take_down` returns it to them (or, when someone else takes it down and they're full, holds it aside), and a pedestal or frame with something on it can't be removed. `remove_display` (staff, after a report) returns it too, or, when their things are full, puts it in `items.heldAside` (absent until the first), and `returnHeldAside` gives it back in `prepare()`'s commit with their first input that leaves room. With `picture`, it drops `media` and `model` from every piece showing that upload, wherever it is.
- Gifts can be sent back once the server logs `open_gifts`, which creates `items.gifts`. From then on each accepted `give` records a gift (`gift_1`, ...) that its recipient may send back whole with `decline_gift` until `lastDeclineDay`, if the giver has room. `new_day` forgets older ones. Before `open_gifts`, `give` hashes exactly as it did ([decision 0057](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0057-a-gift-can-carry-a-thing-and-its-recipient-can-send-it-back-.md)).

## The town shop

- Nothing about the shop runs until `open_shop`, which needs coins and items open and creates `state.shop`. Until then old logs replay exactly; `src/fixtures/shop-log.ts` pins a log with the shop open, and `shop.test.ts` pins every price.
- Decor (`DECOR_BLOCKS`: `lantern`, `frame`, `fence`, `bench`, and autumn's `hay_bale` and `scarecrow`) is both a block kind and a stack kind. `place` takes one from the placer's things and `remove` gives it to whoever takes it up; free blocks touch nobody's things. A starter home is never built from decor.
- Shop wear (`SHOP_WEAR` in `looks.ts`) is bought once into `shop.wardrobe` and kept. `join` and `profile` refuse shop wear the resident doesn't own (`not_owned`).
- A purchase moves the price out of the purse, the treasury's share (`treasuryShareOf`, rounded down) into the treasury with a line that names nobody, and burns the rest. The share is `shop.treasuryShare`, absent (50%) until the server logs `set_shop_share` to match `SHOP.treasuryShare` (5%), so purchases replay at the share they were made at. A sale to the town mints the price. The town buys only `townBuys(day)`, each kind up to its `perDay` per resident, counted in `shop.today` and reset at `new_day`.
- Once the shop is open the pantry uses `ITEMS.shopPantry` and `ITEMS.shopStapleMax`. Read it through `pantryNumbers(state)`.
- Seasons (RFC 0017): `shop_buy` refuses stock in `SEASON_STOCK` out of its season (`out_of_season`), and `townBuys(day)` is the rotation followed by `SEASON_BUYS[seasonOf(day)]`. The rotation cycles through `ROTATION_GOODS` and `ROTATION_CROPS`, frozen in the catalog: a longer list would change what the town bought on every past day. The town buys only kinds with a buy order (`SellKind`); a new kind for it to buy gets an order and goes in a season's buys, by a decision of its own. Nothing else reads the season, so seeds, recipes, and decor work in any season. `src/fixtures/autumn-log.ts` pins a log with autumn's things, and `seasons.test.ts` pins its hash, the rotation, and autumn's numbers ([decision 0079](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0079-autumn-s-numbers-pumpkins-pumpkin-pie-and-soup-hay-bales-and.md)).
- Townsfolk never shop (`not_eligible`).

## Building (RFC 0016)

- Paths and floors live in `state.ground` (tile key to kind), absent until the first `lay` or `build` lays one. Ground never blocks: `isSolid` reads blocks only. It goes under blocks, hearths, and anyone standing there. `release` refuses (`plot_has_blocks`) while a plot has ground, as it does with blocks.
- A ground kind with a cost takes it from the layer's things and gives it back to whoever lifts it, like decor. Free kinds touch nobody's things and work before items open.
- Furniture (`FURNITURE_BLOCKS` in `types.ts`, the catalog's `furniture` role) is a block kind and a stack kind, held and placed like decor (`isHeldBlock`), and every piece blocks walking. A new piece is a catalog entry with the role `furniture` in the decor › furniture family, and its name on the end of `BLOCK_KINDS`, never in `FREE_BLOCKS` or `BUILDING_BLOCKS`. It never becomes a good (a good is a made thing with an id and a maker, not a stack). A furniture craft takes no label and counts toward `craftPerDay`.
- `build` names a plot and counts tiles from its north-west corner. What the plan gets wrong refuses all of it; what moves (someone standing, a hearth, a tile already holding something) is skipped and reported. Removals and lifts go first and pay for what the plan places. Its events are the single actions' own plus one `inventory` (`built`) with the net change.
- `src/fixtures/build-log.ts` pins a log that gathers, makes furniture, places it, lays and lifts ground, and builds a plan, on top of the presence log.
- A Town Hall `commons_build` lays and lifts ground and places decor and furniture in the Commons from nobody's things, so it never touches an inventory and works before items open ([decision 0101](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0101-a-town-hall-build-lays-paths-and-places-decor-and-furniture-.md)). Only proposals with paths carry `ground` and `lift`, and `town_built` has `laid` and `lifted` only when there are some, so older builds close with the events they always made. `src/fixtures/commons-log.ts` pins a log of Commons builds on top of the build log.

## The market

- Nothing about the market runs until `open_market`, which needs the shop open and creates `state.market`. Until then old logs replay exactly; `src/fixtures/market-log.ts` pins a log with trades.
- A listed lot leaves the seller's things and lives in `market.listings` (escrow) until `unlist_item` gives it back (if there's room) or `buy_listing` moves it to the buyer. Listing needs a hearth and burns `MARKET.listingFee`. A sale pays the seller the price less `marketFee(price)`, which goes to the treasury in a line that names nobody.
- `listed`, `unlisted`, `listing_sold`, and `listing_removed` are public; `listing_sold` never names the buyer. Coin and inventory events stay private.
- `remove_listing` (staff, after a report) gives the lot back to its seller, or, when their things are full, marks the listing `takenDown` and keeps it out of `listingsOf` and `stallOf` until the seller collects it with `unlist_item`. Nothing goes past `inventoryMax`, and no coins move.
- Townsfolk never trade (`not_eligible`). The server adds two gates the sim can't see: time in Terrakin and karma for listing, and blocks for buying.

## Bounties and grants

- Nothing about bounties runs until `open_bounties`, which needs coins open and creates `state.bounties`. `grant` and `bounty` proposals are refused until then too. Until then old logs replay exactly; `src/fixtures/bounties-log.ts` pins a log with a bounty paid, one cancelled, and a grant.
- A bounty's reward leaves its poster's purse (or the treasury, for a town bounty) when it's posted and is held in the bounty until it's paid, cancelled, or expires. `bountyHeld` is part of the supply identity: `sum(coins) + treasury + bountyHeld == minted - burned`. `expectSupplyHolds` checks it.
- A resident's bounty is paid by its poster (`confirm_bounty`), a town bounty by a maintainer through the server (`confirm_town_bounty`, never the claimant, the proposer, or either's household). `to` must name the claimant in both, so a payout can't race a change of claimant. A maintainer sends a town bounty's claimant back with `reopen_bounty`.
- A passed `grant` doesn't pay at close: its coins go into a bounty with `grant: true`, held by its resident and marked done, and `confirm_town_bounty` releases it (coin reason `grant`, event `grant_paid`). It can't be claimed, dropped, or reopened, and doesn't expire.
- Grants and town bounties take only what the treasury holds above `ECONOMY.budgetReserve` (`treasurySpare`), when filed and when they close.
- Posting counts toward the poster's `giveCap`, paying toward the claimant's `receiveCap` (an owner pair skips it). Grants and town bounties come from the treasury and skip the caps. Townsfolk neither post nor claim.
- Open or claimed bounties expire at `new_day` once `expiresDay` comes; done ones wait. Only the `BOUNTIES.keepFinished` most recently finished bounties (by `closedDay`) stay in the world.
- `bountyMoves` and `postBountyProblem` run the commands' own checks without committing, so views offer only what the sim would accept.
- `bounty_posted` carries the bounty's words; the server strips them on the wire, like proposal titles.

## Hosted events

- Nothing about events runs until the first `schedule_event` or `schedule_town_event` creates `state.events` (`{nextId, list}`, ids `e_1`, ...). Until then old logs replay exactly; `src/fixtures/events-log.ts` pins a log that schedules, calls off, starts, samples, joins, ends, and voids events, the town's included.
- Hosting needs `townEligibility` (the Town Hall's rule) and a plot the host can build on, or the Commons. A start is on a whole minute, on a UTC day from `state.day` to 14 days on; the server checks the hour's notice, since the sim has no clock. At most 2 events per host on the calendar, one Commons event per host within 7 days, and 15 minutes between events at one place.
- The server logs an event's life: `event_start` (never before its day), `event_tick {event, slot}` at each 5-minute mark (each mark once, in order, none past the end), and `event_end`. A tick adds one to `seen` for each resident online in the area (the plot and 2 tiles around it), never the host or townsfolk. `attendNeeded` is 2 samples, or a third of them if that's more, counting at most 35.
- A Commons booking moves `EVENTS.deposit` out of the host's purse into the event (`event_deposit`). `eventHeld` is part of the supply identity. It comes back (`event_refund`) when `event_end` finds 3 attendees outside the host's household (`sameHousehold`), when the host calls it off before the event's day, or on `void_event`; otherwise it's burned.
- `join_event` puts a resident on the tile the server logged with it (`x`, `y`), which `joinTile` picks: on a plot someone lives on, where `visit` lands a visitor (`visitTile`), and in the Commons the free tile nearest the middle. The sim checks the logged tile (in the area, no block or building, no hearth, no other online resident) and never plans. An input without a tile, as in older logs, lands on the free tile nearest the plot's middle (`landingFor`), as it always did ([decision 0103](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0103-join-event-lands-a-guest-where-visit-lands-a-visitor-logged-.md)). One already in the area is refused (`already_joined`) unless the input brings them back online, which `prepare` tells `check` (`rejoining`).
- `release` calls off the events on its plot, `unshare_plot` the unshared co-owner's there, and `new_day` drops events that finished more than 30 days before.
- `event_scheduled` carries where and when, never the words. `event_ticked` describes a sample; the server keeps it off the wire.

## Coins

- Nothing about coins runs until `open_economy`, which creates `state.economy`. Until then old logs replay exactly (`src/fixtures/pre-economy-log.ts` pins one with days, townsfolk, and a Town Hall). `src/fixtures/post-economy-log.ts` pins a log with coins moving, so a change to `ECONOMY` or a coin rule that would replay the live log differently fails a test. `ownerPairs` and `maintainers` are top-level keys, absent until set, and can be set before coins open. `ownerPairDays` records the day each pair first appeared; a pair skips the gift caps only from the day after. `add_owner_pair` and `remove_owner_pair` change one pair and leave the world exactly as `set_owner_pairs` with the whole new list would, so each link change logs one small input ([decision 0042](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0042-owner-pairs-reach-the-sim-one-pair-at-a-time.md)). Keep them equivalent; a test compares the hashes step by step.
- `sum(economy.coins) + economy.treasury + bountyHeld + eventHeld == economy.minted - economy.burned` after every input. `economy.test.ts` checks it after each one.
- The allowance and the welcome gift are bookkeeping in `prepare()`'s commit, like `lastActiveDay`: the welcome gift is worked out before the command commits and is only paid in full (otherwise the resident waits in `economy.owed`, which `new_day` pays in order), and the allowance pays whenever a resident's own input (not `join` or `leave`) leaves them on their hearth. `home` while already home is accepted only when it collects the allowance.
- Purses are private. `state.economy` holds every purse and ledger, so the public world snapshot must drop it, keeping only the treasury (`treasuryOf`). A `coins` event belongs to its `residentId` alone: the server sends it only to that resident, never in the public broadcast, and never in another resident's action response or socket echo. A gift returns two `coins` events from one input, one for each side, so the giver's response must drop the receiver's (it carries their balance). `treasury` events and `economy_opened` are public. `owner_pairs_set`, `owner_pair_added`, `owner_pair_removed`, and `maintainers_set` stay on the server. Use `purseOf` for `GET /v1/purse`.
- The treasury's history is public: townsfolk budgets and their returns are one line each for all townsfolk together, and only welcome gifts name a resident. Their difference is the day's net of townsfolk tips and gifts to townsfolk, so the server keeps gifts with townsfolk on either side out of the public gift list and the public `gift` event, leaving that net unattributed.
- `daily_awards {day, awards}` mints coins the server counted for a day that has ended (appreciation, [decision 0055](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0055-karma-is-scored-from-90-days-of-appreciation-outside-the-sim.md)). Each day is paid at most once and in order (`economy.awardedDay`, absent until the first). `ECONOMY.appreciationCap` may go up, never down, since replay checks every logged award against it.
- Townsfolk never save: at `new_day` a townsfolk resident's whole purse goes back to the treasury, including coins they held before joining the list, and leaving the list hands it back at once.
- Players see "coins", never "token".

## Pets

- A pet lives on its owner's resident (`pet`), absent until the first `adopt_pet`, so old logs keep their hash. `src/fixtures/pets-log.ts` pins a log that uses all four commands.
- One pet each, for good, and only with a hearth. Adopting is free; `rename_pet` is free once a world day (`renamedDay`); `groom_pet` burns `PETS.groomFee` (reason `groom`), and townsfolk never groom.
- `treat_pet` takes one of the giver's produce (`CROPS`, inventory reason `treat`) for any resident's pet, once a pet a day, and records who gave what on the pet (`treat`), which keeps it happy until the day changes.
- Where a pet stands, and whether it's awake, is never state: clients draw it. Pats aren't sim inputs either; they're social rows in the server.
- A pet's name is resident text. The sim checks its length; the server cleans and filters it before it's logged, and `pet_adopted` and `pet_renamed` go out marked `untrusted`.

## Party games

- Nothing about games runs until the first `open_table`, which creates `state.games`; no switch input is needed, since only new commands touch it. `src/fixtures/games-log.ts` pins a log with a whole Hearth race and a whole Lowest lantern, and `games.test.ts` its hash.
- A round is sealed: `table.sealed` holds each `decide` until `close_round`, and no event carries a choice before then (`decided` says only who). `round_closed` reveals them all at once, in seat order, with `null` for a seat that played the default. The order choices came in never matters.
- The server stamps `open_table`, `sit`, and `start_game` with its clock (`at`, in ms), `open_table` with its salt, and a `start_game` from anyone but the first seat with `free` once the start grace has passed; it decides when a round closes, and the sim never compares a time. `openedAt`, `roundAt`, and `readyAt` (when the table last came to have enough seats) are for the server's runner and views. The table keeps the salt as `secret`, in the hashed world so `/v1/health`'s hash can't be used to guess sealed choices; only `game_over` shows it. Its key must sort after `sealed`, since FNV-1a can be run backwards over known bytes: a secret before the choices would let the hashes before and after a `decide` give the move away. `games.test.ts` checks the order.
- Tables stand at `gameTableTiles`, which don't stop walkers. A new table takes the first spot with no table and no block, and only a resident with a hearth opens one; `sit` puts a resident on the open tile nearest it. The first seat that isn't townsfolk starts the game (`starterOf`), townsfolk never do, and a table with nobody left but townsfolk closes in the same `stand`.
- Ratings ([decision 0096](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0096-game-ratings-are-whole-number-elo-kept-in-the-sim-between-se.md)) are whole numbers, worked out at `game_over` from places, pair by pair within each ladder (`people:` or `agents:`, then the pace), scaled by the number of rated seats of that kind. Who's rated is fixed at `start_game`: residents who could vote in the Town Hall (`townEligibility`), not sharing a household with another seat (`oneHousehold`), under `GAMES.ratedPerDay`, in pairs under `GAMES.pairPerDay` and `GAMES.pairPerWeek`. `new_day` resets the day's counts; the week's (`games.week`, from a Monday by the UTC calendar) start again with the first counted game of a later week. Changing a number in `GAMES` or `GAME_RULES` changes how logged games replay, so it needs a logged switch.
- Finished tables move to `games.finished`, the newest `GAMES.keepFinished` kept, with their places, rating changes, and `endedAt` (the last `close_round`'s `at`).

## Adding a kind

A new kind of thing is an entry in `src/catalog.ts`, on the end of `WRITTEN`:

1. One entry: a `fruit(...)` or `crop(...)` call for something that grows (it makes the seed too, and a fruit's jam comes from the jam family recipe), or an `entry(...)` with its name and plural, family, role, and, as it needs them, a recipe or a shop price and seasons.
2. Its look: a template and colors (`produce`, `sprig`, `bloom`, `jar`), or `drawn` with a picture under its id in `ui/src/item-art.ts`.
3. Decor and furniture are blocks too: their id goes on the end of `BLOCK_KINDS` in `types.ts`, with a color in `BLOCK_COLORS` and their 3D and plot photo pictures.

The tests that run over every entry cover the rest: ids, families, names, looks, seeds and crops that pair up, recipes from things that stack, a jam for every fruit, and a picture for every kind. Pomegranates and the jack-o'-lantern were added this way ([decision 0085](../docs/knowledge/decisions/0085-furniture-keeps-its-own-role-in-decor-furniture-and-pomegran.md)). A new kind never joins a frozen list or what the town buys by being added: those are their own decisions. A new find lies nowhere until a new spawn table starts at a logged switch (`FIND_SPAWNS` is frozen), and `gather.test.ts` fails on a find that no table spawns. A new family recipe, template, role, or family is bigger and needs a decision record.

## Adding a command

1. Add it to `Command` in `types.ts`, plus any new `WorldEvent` and `RejectionCode`.
2. Implement the case in `check()` in `apply.ts`: validate everything and return a rejection, or return a closure that mutates and returns events. Do all reads in the check, not in the closure.
3. Test accept and every rejection path in `apply.test.ts`, plus a replay check if it touches new state.
4. Expose it in `protocol/` (schema and `SKILL.md`, then `pnpm gen`) and render its events in `client/src/mirror.ts`.
